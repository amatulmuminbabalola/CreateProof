CreateProof

About the Project

CreateProof is a project management platform designed to help creative professionals manage their projects, revision requests, client approvals, and payment records in one place.

Target Users

- Graphic designers
- Photographers and videographers
- Video editors
- Writers and content creators
- Other freelance creative professionals

Problem We Are Solving

Creative professionals often manage projects through scattered WhatsApp conversations, making it difficult to track revisions, approvals, project progress, and outstanding payments.

Our Solution

CreateProof aims to provide a simple and organised workflow for managing creative projects from the initial request to final approval and payment tracking.

Planned MVP Features

- Project creation and tracking
- Revision request records
- Client approval tracking
- Payment status tracking
- A simple project dashboard
